Our Story

Crafted with
intention, served
with warmth

Silver Spoon Café was born from a simple belief — that a meal shared is a moment remembered. Since 2009, our kitchen has been guided by seasonal ingredients, honest technique, and a deep respect for the people who sit at our tables.

Our chefs work closely with local farms and artisan producers, ensuring every dish carries the flavour of its origin. From the first coffee of the morning to the last dessert of the evening, we cook with care.
